Privacy

No analytics, telemetry or accounts. Dblore has no servers of its own.

What the app sends over the network

  • Update checks. Dblore fetches the update feed (appcast.xml) from GitHub Pages and downloads updates from GitHub Releases. You can turn background checks off in Settings.
  • Your databases. Connections go to the servers you configure, nowhere else.
  • AI assistant. Only when you use it, and only to the provider you chose with your own key or sign-in. Table and column names, types and keys are sent automatically. Query text and error messages are sent only after you review and press Send. Dblore does not send row data on its own.
  • Local AI. Ollama, LM Studio, mlx_lm.server and on-device MLX models keep your prompts on your machine. Downloading an MLX model fetches it from huggingface.co.
  • Nothing else. No usage, crash or install reporting.

What stays on your Mac

  • Passwords and AI keys are stored in the Keychain.
  • Query history, saved chats, settings and your notebooks stay on your Mac.
  • Local data backups (.dblorebackup) exclude secrets: an export is scanned for them and refused if one is found.
  • Settings > Data lets you export, import or clear local data.

This website

  • Hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses.
  • No analytics and no cookies. Your theme choice is kept in local storage.
  • The version in the header is read from this site's own appcast.xml, a same-origin request with no third party involved.
  • The Geist font is loaded from Google Fonts.

Questions

Dblore is open source, so every claim here can be checked in the code. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
